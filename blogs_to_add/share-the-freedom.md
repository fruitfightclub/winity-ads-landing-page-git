# Share the Freedom: How Winity Loop Rewards You and Your Friends!

*   [February 2, 2026](https://winity.life/2026/02/02/)
*   3:10 pm

## Financial freedom is better when it’s shared.

Winity Loop is our referral program designed to reward you and your friends for building a world without financial borders.

## What is Winity Loop?

Winity Loop is our built-in referral program designed to be seamless. By sharing your unique referral link, you and your friends earn together. Once your friend signs up and reaches a total spend of **$10**, we credit **$2** directly to both of your balances. The $10 requirement can be met through a single transaction or spread across several smaller ones.

## How It Works

1

### Share your link

Find your unique referral code in the Referral section of the Winity app and share it with your friends.

2

### Your friend joins

Your friend signs up and completes their identity verification (KYC).

3

### Spend at their pace

Once they have spent a total of $10 using Winity Life, the reward is triggered.

4

### Earn together

$2 is added to both your balances instantly, ready to be used toward your next purchase or transaction.

## Getting Started

1.   1 Download Winity Life: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/winity-life) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.winity.life) 
2.   2 Get your link: Tap the Referral tab in your app to find your unique code. 
3.   3 Share: Send your link to friends, family, or your professional network. 
4.   4 The Loop completes: Once your friend passes KYC and spends a cumulative $10, the bonus is triggered. 
5.   5 Monitor progress: Track your successful referrals and rewards in real-time within the app.
