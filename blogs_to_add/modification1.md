Title: Winity Life Unveils “Christmas That Keeps Giving” – Winity Life

URL Source: https://winity.life/winity-life-unveils-christmas-that-keeps-giving/

Markdown Content:
**Warsaw Poland, – December 22, 2025**, This Christmas, Winity Life is reframing the idea of a holiday gift. Instead of something that ends once unwrapped, the company is introducing an experience that continues to deliver value well beyond the season. Launching December 19, 2025, “Christmas That Keeps Giving” is designed to convert a simple deposit into ongoing spending power, rewarding both new and existing users at a time when every extra dollar counts.

At the heart of the campaign is a clear proposition: make a qualifying deposit, receive an instant bonus, and enjoy the flexibility to spend through the Winity Card across every day and festive categories. The reward is credited immediately to the Winity wallet and remains valid for card usage until March 31, 2026, ensuring that the benefit extends past Christmas and into the new year.

**How the campaign works**

*   · New users who complete KYC and make their first deposit of $10 receive an instant $5 bonus, giving them $15 ready to spend.
*   · Existing users who make a single deposit of $25 receive the same $5 bonus, unlocking $30 in total spending value.

The bonus is automatically credited and can be used exclusively via the Winity Card, encouraging seamless, cashless spending during the holidays and beyond.

**Designed for real-world festive spending**To sustain engagement, the campaign highlights popular spending destinations such as online marketplaces and subscription platforms, reinforcing the message that Winity Life fits naturally into daily life. Whether it is holiday shopping, entertainment, or routine expenses, the Winity Card becomes the channel through which the reward keeps circulating.

From December 19, users will encounter the campaign directly within the app, led by a festive splash screen and seasonal messaging that signals the start of the offer and guides participation.

| **Feature** | **New Users** | **Existing Users** |

“**Christmas That Keeps Giving**” positions Winity Life as more than a holiday promotion. It is a strategic reminder that smart, simple financial actions can unlock lasting value. Users are encouraged to download or update the Winity Life app, make a deposit, and let their Christmas spending power stretch well into the months ahead.
