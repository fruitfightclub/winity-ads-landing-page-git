[How do I download and install the Winity Life app?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-20aca326a1ff00ae34d0)

**For Android (Google Play Store):**

1.   Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android device.
2.   In the search bar, type **“Winity”**.
3.   Select the official **Winity Life app** from the results.
4.   Tap **Install**.
5.   Once installed, tap **Open** to launch the app.

**For iOS (Apple App Store):**

1.   Open the **App Store** on your iPhone or iPad.
2.   In the search bar, type “**Winity**”.
3.   Select the official **Winity Life app** from the results.
4.   **Tap Get** and confirm with your Apple ID, Face ID, or Touch ID.
5.   Once installed, tap **Open** to launch the app.

[What information is required to register an account?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-c8ebd726a1ff00ae34d0)

**Registration**

1.    Enter your phone number in the app.
2.    Enter the OTP received on the given phone number to access the app.

**Identity Verification**

1.    Verify your email ID with the OTP sent to your inbox.
2.    Provide personal details — name, date of birth, and address.
3.    Upload your identification document (National Identity Card, Driving License, or Passport).
4.    Take a selfie (Face Capture) as prompted by the application.
5.    Submit the documents for verification.
6.    Wait for the documents to get approved
7.   Once approved, your application will proceed for card issuance.

[How long does Identity verification (KYC) take?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-d97e8b26a1ff00ae34d0)

Identity verification (KYC) is usually completed within minutes and may vary depending on review requirements.

[In which countries are the Winity Card available?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-40e57696a1ff00ae34d0)

The Winity card is broadly available across many [countries](https://winity.life/countries-and-regions/).

[Are there any restrictions on who can use the Winity Card?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-bd3a42c6a1ff00ae34d0)

Anyone who meets the following criteria can apply for a Winity Card:

1.   You are **18 years or older**.
2.   You have successfully completed the **KYC (Know Your Customer) verification** process.
3.   You are a resident of a **supported (non-restricted) country**.

[Where can I find detailed instructions on using the Winity Card and Winity Life app?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-b3c82696a1ff00ae34d0)

You can refer to the **Winity.Life User Guide** for step-by-step instructions on registration, funding, card usage, and account management.

[What documents are needed to complete KYC?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-20aca326a1ff00ae8d2e)

Following documents can be used to complete the Identity Verification (KYC):

1.   Passport
2.   Driving License
3.   National Identity Card

[How secure is my personal information during KYC?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-c8ebd726a1ff00ae8d2e)

Your information will be kept confidential in accordance with the applicable data protection laws and regulatory requirements in Hongkong. All data shared during the KYC process is encrypted and stored securely in compliance.

[Can I update my KYC information later?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-d97e8b26a1ff00ae8d2e)

Yes, you can update your KYC details at a later stage. However, please note that a card will only be issued once your Identity Verification (KYC) has been successfully completed.

[How do I create my Winity Card in the app?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-20aca326a1ff00aedfc9)

**Registration**

1.   Enter your phone number in the app.
2.   Enter the OTP received on the given phone number to access the app.

**Identity Verification**

1.   Verify your email ID with the OTP sent to your inbox.
2.   Provide personal details — name, date of birth, and address.
3.   Upload your identification document (National Identity Card, Driving License, or Passport).
4.   Take a selfie (Face Capture) as prompted by the application.
5.   Submit the documents for verification.
6.   Wait for the documents to get approved.
7.   Once approved, your application will proceed for card issuance.

[Is the card virtual, physical, or both?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-c8ebd726a1ff00aedfc9)

Winity offer two types of cards, **Exclusive and Executive**, and both are available in virtual and physical formats. The Exclusive card comes with a free virtual card, while the Executive card includes both a free virtual card and a premium metal physical card.

[Where can I use the Winity Card?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-d97e8b26a1ff00aedfc9)

The Winity Card can be used across **150 million merchants globally** where Visa cards are accepted.

[How do I add the card to Google Pay?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-40e57696a1ff00aedfc9)

1.   Open the **Google Pay** app on your phone.
2.   Tap on **Add a Payment Method** or **Add Pay with Card** option.
3.   Enter your **Winity Card details** (card number, expiry date, CVV).
4.   Verify your card through the OTP sent to your registered phone or email.
5.   Once verified, your Winity Card will be added to Google Pay and ready for use.

Your Winity Card can now be used seamlessly for contactless payments and online transactions through Google Pay.

[What is a Wallet Address?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-20aca326a1ff00af3942)

A Wallet Address is your unique blockchain address for receiving funds. In the Winity Life app, your wallet addresses can be fetched from the **Deposit** option on the main home page, with a separate address shown for each network.

[How do I check my wallet balance and transaction history?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-c8ebd726a1ff00af3942)

1.   You can view your balance directly on the main home page, where the balance for each card is shown under the respective card.

2.   Your recent transactions also appear in the activity section. For more details, go to the Transactions section, where you can filter by card, time period, or type of transaction such as deposits, spends, and rewards.

[How can I add funds to Winity Life App?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-d97e8b26a1ff00af3942)

The Winity Life app supports deposits through traditional methods like bank transfers and supported digital assets. Stablecoins such as USDT and USDC are supported with no additional deposit fees. Other supported assets may be subject to network or conversion fees depending on the asset and blockchain network.

Winity Life app:

Open the **Winity Life app** and go to the **Deposit** section on the home page.

Select the network you want to use (a separate wallet address is shown for each supported network).

Copy the wallet address provided or scan the QR code displayed in the app.

Transfer your chosen digital currency to this address.

![Image 4: ⚠️](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/16.0.1/svg/26a0.svg)_Important:_ Always ensure you select the correct **network and token** before making a deposit. Using the wrong network may result in failed or lost transactions.

(Please note that when you transfer digital assets from an external wallet or exchange, sender-side blockchain gas fees may apply. These fees are charged by the network, not by Winity Life, and may result in a slightly lower received amount.)

[Which digital currencies are supported by the Winity Life App?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-40e57696a1ff00af3942)

Winity Life supports a wide range of digital currencies:

1.    Stablecoins: USDT, USDC
2.    Non-stable assets: WCO, ETH, BSC, POL, Tron and SOL.
3.    Supported networks: Ethereum, W Chain, Polygon, Tron, Solana, Binance Smart Chain.

[How can I withdraw my funds from the Winity Card?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-2996ec76a1ff00af3942)

You can withdraw funds at any ATM worldwide where Visa is accepted. The Winity Life App does not support withdrawal of funds in the form of digital assets.

[What happens when I deposit digital assets into the Winity Life App?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-454e1746a1ff00af3942)

Once your transfer is completed, your digital assets will appear in the Deposit section of the App. Your available balance will then be shown in USD for use with your Winity Card.

(Please Note: network gas fees may apply depending on the asset and network used)

[If I want to request my deposit back into digital assets, what can I do?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-73aae606a1ff00af3942)

Deposits in the Winity Life App are intended for Winity Card use, and withdraws back into digital assets are not supported. However, in exceptional cases, you may contact[Winity Support](https://winity.life/support/) to submit a review request. Our team will:

1.   Temporarily block your card to protect your balance.
2.   Review the relevant transactions and account activity.
3.   Confirm the eligible refundable amount after applicable fees and charges.
4.   Process the refund once you approve the confirmation email.

Following conditions will be applied while processing refunds in digital assets:

1.   Only the most recent deposit amount will be considered for review.
2.   A request will only be accepted if it is submitted immediately after the deposit. If any card transaction occurs after the deposit, the request cannot be processed.
3.   n case of a refund request, the final decision will rest with Winity Life.

[Are there any fees for depositing non-stable digital assets?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-cf871b06a1ff00af3942)

Yes, a 5% fee is applied when depositing non-stable digital assets into your Winity wallet. This fee covers network and conversion charges associated with these transactions.

[Can I use the Winity Card for online and in-store payments?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-20aca326a1ff00b05ba4)

Winity Life is a versatile platform that caters to all your payment requirements. Users can conveniently spend in USD with Winity Card. Choose from deposit options like Cash, USDT, USDC, and Crypto. With the Winity Card, you can make instant purchases at over 150 million merchants globally.

[What happens if a payment is declined?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-c8ebd726a1ff00b05ba4)

If a payment attempt is declined, we will notify you immediately. You may retry the transaction with the Winity Card after taking the following precautionary steps.

1.   Ensure that your Winity Card has sufficient available balance.
2.   Verify that your Winity Card is active, valid, and enabled for the type of transaction.
3.   Check that you have not exceeded any applicable transaction or daily spending limits.

If the issue continues, please contact our customer support team for further assistance.

[How secure are Winity Card transactions?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-d97e8b26a1ff00b05ba4)

Winity Card transactions are protected by advanced encryption and security protocols to keep your payments safe. Each transaction is processed through Visa’s global network, ensuring the highest standards of security and fraud protection.

[What is the exchange rate at which my balance is converted?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-40e57696a1ff00b05ba4)

Your balance is converted at the current market exchange rate at the time of the transaction. Winity automatically applies the prevailing rate to ensure transparency and accuracy when converting between digital assets and USD.

[How many Winity Points do I earn when I use my Winity Card for purchases?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-2996ec76a1ff00b05ba4)

Winity offers attractive rewards on every purchase made using your card:

1.   **Exclusive Card:** Earn **1 Winity Point for every USD 10 spent**
2.   **Executive Card:** Earn **1 Winity Point for every USD 8 spent**

Your rewards are automatically credited as **Winity Points**, which can be viewed and tracked directly in the Winity Life app.

[How is my financial data protected?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-20aca326a1ff00b0b9d1)

Your information will be kept confidential in accordance with the applicable data protection laws and regulatory requirements in Hong Kong. All data shared during the KYC process is encrypted and stored securely in compliance.

[What should I do if a transaction happens that I have not done?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-c8ebd726a1ff00b0b9d1)

If you notice any suspicious activity, block your card immediately through the app or by contacting our support team at **support@winity.life**. Report the details to us as soon as possible so we can investigate and assist you further. Please share the transaction details with us so we can review the case and raise it with Visa for further investigation.

[How do I set up a security PIN?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-d97e8b26a1ff00b0b9d1)

You will be asked to create a PIN during registration. If you wish to change it later, go to **Settings > Privacy & Security > Change PIN**.

[Can I set biometric authentication for performing transactions?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-40e57696a1ff00b0b9d1)

For online transactions, you would be required to provide biometric authentication on Winity Life App. You can enable biometrics under **Settings > Privacy & Security > Enable/Disable Biometrics**.

[How to Freeze my Card?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-37282a56a1ff00b0b9d1)

1.   Open the Winity Life app and go to the Home Page.
2.   Locate the card you want to freeze.
3.   Tap the “Freeze” icon displayed under the card.
4.   Confirm your action when prompted.
5.   Once confirmed, your card will freeze immediately and cannot be used for transactions.

[How can I provide feedback on the app and card experience?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-2996ec76a1ff00b0b9d1)

We invite you to share your experience through feedback forms from time to time. You can also provide feedback anytime by using our[WhatsApp](https://wa.link/wxto1u) chatbot available through the Help menu or visit our Members Services portal online, available through the Help menu.

[Who do I contact for technical support or issues?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-57f75c16a1ff00b0b9d1)

For any technical support or issues, you can reach us at **support@winity.life**. You can also connect with us instantly through our Members Services portal online or WhatsApp chatbot available in the Help menu.

[What is 3D Secure (3DS)?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-c8495a46a1ff00b0b9d1)

3D Secure is an extra layer of protection for online card transactions. It helps confirm that the person making the payment is the authorised cardholder, keeping your purchases safe from unauthorised use.

[How does 3D Secure work with Winity Cards?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-841c5a26a1ff00b0b9d1)

When you make an online payment using your Winity Card, you’ll receive a notification from the Winity Life app asking you to approve the transaction.

Simply verify the payment using your biometric authentication (fingerprint or face ID) to complete the purchase securely.

[Do I need to enter an OTP for 3D Secure transactions?](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-7faa8db6a1ff00b0b9d1)

No. Winity’s 3D Secure system is OTP-free. Instead, you’ll authenticate transactions directly through the Winity Life app using biometrics, providing a faster and more secure experience.

[Winter Offer - The Christmas That Keeps Giving (Ended)](https://winity.life/faqs/#collapse-f97b6006a1ff00b17952)

**1. When does the "Christmas That Keeps Giving" campaign run?**

The campaign begins on December 20, 2025. All bonus funds received are valid for use through March 31, 2026.

**2. How do I qualify for the $5.00 bonus?**

Eligibility depends on your account status:

*   **New Users:** Successfully complete KYC verification and make a first-time deposit of at least USD 10.00.
*   **Existing Users:** Make a single successful deposit of at least USD 25.00 during the campaign period.

_Note: Eligibility may vary by jurisdiction based on local laws and internal policies._

**3. When will I receive my reward?**

The $5.00 reward is credited to your Winity wallet immediately upon a successful qualifying deposit. Only one bonus is applicable per user.

**4. How can I use my bonus funds?**

Bonus funds are strictly for card-based transactions, including online shopping or point-of-sale (POS) purchases using your Winity Card.

**5. Can I withdraw the bonus or my deposit to an external wallet?**

No. All granted bonuses are for spending via the Winity Card only. Direct withdrawals to external wallets are currently not supported.

**6. What happens if I don't use the bonus by March 31, 2026?**

Any unused bonus funds remaining in your account after March 31, 2026, will be forfeited and removed from your balance.
