How Winity Life protects your wealth and payments without SMS friction.

What is OTP-free 3D Secure?

Traditional cards send SMS OTPs, which fail constantly when traveling. Winity replaces SMS with in-app biometrics (Face ID / Fingerprint) to verify transactions instantly and securely.

How fast is the card issued?

Virtual cards are generated instantly upon finishing Step 2 (Facial KYC). Once verified, you can link it directly to Google Pay and start spending immediately.

Which stablecoins are supported?

We support USDT and USDC at 0% platform loading fees. These stablecoins can be loaded from Tron (TRC-20), Solana, Polygon, Ethereum, and BSC networks.

Are there any monthly staking requirements?

No. Winity Life does not require you to stake, buy, or lock up any native tokens to unlock benefits. Your capital remains fully liquid and in your control.
